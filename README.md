# ArtLens

**ArtLens** is an AI-powered art discovery and learning app that helps users understand artwork in a simple, interactive, and engaging way.

Users can take a picture of an artwork or upload an image, and ArtLens analyzes it to provide useful information such as:

* Artwork title and artist
* Art style or movement
* Year and medium
* Simple explanation of the artwork
* Visual elements such as color, mood, brushwork, and composition
* AI confidence score

ArtLens also includes gamification features such as XP, quizzes, collections, achievements, streaks, and an Art Passport to encourage users to keep discovering and learning about art.

## Benefits

* Makes art easier for beginners to understand
* Turns art education into an interactive experience
* Helps users recognize different art styles
* Encourages learning through games and challenges
* Makes museum visits and art exploration more engaging
* Provides transparent AI results using confidence scores
* Helps users build their own personal art collection

ArtLens combines **AI, education, photography, and gamification** to make learning about art more accessible and enjoyable.
